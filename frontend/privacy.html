TriVex Privacy & Security Information

This document describes data handling, permissions, and security practices in TriVex.

What TriVex Does

TriVex is an explainable browser protection tool. It evaluates web addresses locally in your browser using a combination of deterministic rules (System A), weighted risk scoring (System B), and a hybrid decision engine (System C).

What Stays On Your Device

  • All URL evaluations run locally: The addresses you visit or analyze are evaluated strictly within your browser.
  • Zero external transmission: TriVex does not send visited URLs, page titles, or scan results to external servers or cloud services.
  • Zero history tracking: TriVex does not log or persist a permanent history of the websites you browse.
  • Local storage only: User preferences (e.g. Family Shield toggle) and temporary page context counters are kept strictly in local browser storage.

What TriVex Never Does

  • It never reads, intercepts, or stores password inputs, form field contents, or personal credentials.
  • It never fetches or opens suspicious URLs in the background.
  • It never introduces third-party advertising or commercial tracking scripts.

Permissions Used & Justification

  • activeTab: Allows TriVex to read the web address of the active tab only when you explicitly click "Analyze current page".
  • tabs: Allows the background service worker to redirect navigations to local warning pages (block.html and family_shield.html) when high-confidence phishing or adult content is detected.
  • storage: Stores user preferences (such as enabling or disabling Family Shield) and rolling page context counters locally.

Protection Scope & Limitations

URL-only and structural checks look for known phishing indicators such as IP-based hostnames, credential-harvesting patterns over unencrypted HTTP, and suspicious character groupings. However, URL-based checks cannot detect a compromised legitimate service. "No high-risk indicators detected" does not mean a website is guaranteed safe. Always verify site authenticity before entering credentials.
